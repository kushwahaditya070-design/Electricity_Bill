01 — THE PREMISEENERGY, MADE UNDERSTANDABLE

Small signals.
Big picture.

Electricity is measured in units. Understanding it takes a little more.

This project explores the relationship between household appliance usage, monthly operating hours and tariff rates — translating those signals into an estimated electricity bill through a trained regression model.
